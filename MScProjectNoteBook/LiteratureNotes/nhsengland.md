## **Summary**

- Describes updated NHS Cancer Waiting Time Standards:
    - **Faster Diagnosis Standard (28 days)**
    - **31‑day decision‑to‑treat to treatment**
    - **62‑day referral‑to‑treatment**
- Provides operational guidance on:
    - Pathway redesign
    - Monitoring via NCWTMDS
    - Use of best‑practice timed pathways
- Highlights the need for **consistent data recording** and **timely reporting** across systems. 



## **Relevance**

- Sets the regulatory framework for your pathway analytics.
- Confirms that Trusts need **integrated, real-time operational data** to meet national standards.
- Directly informs your KPI definitions and event data model.



## **Critical Evaluation**

### **Strengths**

- Authoritative national guidance.
- Aligns perfectly with your study’s context and metrics.

### **Weaknesses**

- Describes policy targets but **does not provide tools**.
- Leaves the integration challenge to local Trusts.



## **Artefact Implications**

- Your IDR must map all events to NCWTMDS definitions.
- Dashboard must surface breach‑risk signals aligned to these standards.
- Provides KPI framework for evaluation.
