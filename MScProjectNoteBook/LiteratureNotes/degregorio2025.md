## **Summary**

- Highlights delays across the typical oncology workflow:
    - Scheduling delays
    - Referral bottlenecks
    - Communication breakdowns
    - Data silos between imaging, pathology, labs
- Notes that lack of integrated systems leads to **60–90 day delays** from screening to treatment.
- Proposes **workflow design + integrated technology** as the key lever for improving timeliness. 


## **Relevance to Study**

- Provides direct evidence that oncology workflows are fragmented and that integration is essential for reducing delays.
- Strongly supports the rationale for building an IDR + operational dashboard.



## **Critical Evaluation**

### **Strengths**

- Clear explanation of workflow barriers relevant to NHS oncology pathways.
- Strong emphasis on integration, real‑time alerts, and removing silos.

### **Weaknesses**

- Non‑peer‑reviewed (LinkedIn), though written by a recognised healthcare IT professional.
- More descriptive than empirical.
- Lacks population‑level data beyond cited examples.



## **Implications for Artefact**

- Your system must:
    - Provide **automated referral status** and actionable alerts.
    - Integrate multiple systems (MDT, clinics, treatments).
    - Track pathway intervals and help collapse timelines.



## **Contribution to Literature Gap**

- Demonstrates general awareness of workflow issues,  
    but _no robust, evaluated, NHS‑specific operational analytics tools_ exist.
